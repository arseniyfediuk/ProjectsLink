Huawei Student Tech Conference (STC) 2026 – Digital Energy
Project Overview
This repository contains the presentation and technical documentation for a hybrid digital energy system developed for the Huawei Student Tech Conference (STC) 2026. Our 3-member engineering team from Lviv Polytechnic National University reached the national semifinals in Kyiv, where we pitched this solution in English to an expert jury.

System Architecture & Technical Specifications
The proposed hardware and energy solution integrates multiple power sources and storage mechanisms to ensure uninterrupted power supply.
Primary Generation: Solar panel array.
Secondary/Backup Generation: Gas Combined Heat and Power (CHP) generator.
Energy Storage System (ESS): 15 kW battery capacity.
Power Distribution: High-voltage transformer stages, supporting up to 35 kV battery charging voltage.
Operational Logic: Automated day/night load balancing and power source switching.
